Why we want to Vote

- Foundation of Democracy: Voting is the main way citizens participate in governance.
- Voice in Decision-Making: Your vote helps decide policies that affect education, healthcare, jobs, and the environment.
- Protects Rights: Voting safeguards freedoms and human rights.
- Every Vote Counts: Even a single vote can influence close elections.
- Shapes the Future: Elections determine the direction of communities and nations.
- Historical Struggle: Many groups fought hard for the right to vote.

History of Voting

- Independence (1947): India became the world’s largest democracy.
- Constitutional Foundation (1950): Universal adult suffrage was guaranteed.
- First General Election (1951–52): Over 173 million people were eligible to vote.
- Evolution: EVMs introduced in the 1990s, voter ID cards, and online registration reforms.
